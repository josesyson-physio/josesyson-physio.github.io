Profile

A clinician who started building his own tools

I got tired of paper forms, guesswork and expensive textbooks, so I started building the tools myself.

In the clinic I assess, treat and rehabilitate. Outside it I design apps that let any therapist run standardised assessments on a phone, read posture from a photo, and write clear reports in minutes instead of hours.

Everything I build follows three rules: it works on a cheap phone, it keeps patient data on the device, and it is free for students who can't afford coaching.

Mission

Taking physiotherapy to the next level

Physiotherapy changes lives, but too much of it still runs on paper, memory and access to expensive books. The plan is to give every therapist and every student the tools that bigger professions take for granted.

  1. Phase 1 · Now

    Measure everything

    Standardised assessments on any phone, scored instantly, so progress is shown with numbers instead of impressions.

  2. Phase 2 · Now

    See movement

    Computer vision that reads posture today, and gait and range of motion next, all on the device.

  3. Phase 3 · Building

    Teach without walls

    A free, offline library in a student's own language, so no one fails an exam because they couldn't afford coaching.

  4. Phase 4 · Building

    Connect the clinic and the home

    One ecosystem linking assessment, treatment plans and home exercise, so care continues after the patient walks out.

  5. Phase 5 · Horizon

    Lead with evidence

    Pooled, privacy-safe outcome data that lets physiotherapists prove what works and take their place at the centre of rehabilitation.
